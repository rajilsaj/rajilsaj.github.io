Privacy Policy Draft

Your privacy matters to us.

This draft explains how V's Home Care & Nursing Care LLC handles information shared through this website. Final wording must be reviewed by the company and its legal advisor before launch.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you choose to give us, such as your name, phone, email and ZIP code.
  • General details about the kind of care you are asking about.

What we ask you not to send

Please do not share medical records or detailed health information through the website form or by email. We will discuss health information privately by phone.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and explain care options.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
