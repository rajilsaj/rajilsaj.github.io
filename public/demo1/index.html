About V's

Care that feels like home.

V's Home Care & Nursing Care is a Springfield, Missouri home care provider helping people receive the support they need without leaving the comfort of their own home. We work alongside individuals and their families to make everyday life easier, safer and more comfortable.

“To provide compassionate, respectful care at home — so the people we serve can live with dignity and their families can feel at peace.”

Proposed mission statement · for management approval

Our care philosophy

  • Every person is treated with dignity and respect.
  • Independence is encouraged wherever possible.
  • Families are partners in the care conversation.

Payment & Coverage

Understanding your care options.

V's identifies as a Medicaid-approved provider for certain home care services, and also welcomes questions from private-pay families.

Coverage depends on the program, the person's eligibility and the service. Not every service is covered for every person — we'll help you understand what applies before care begins.

Ask About Coverage Call (417) 434-3554

  • Medicaid

    Ask whether the services you need can be supported through Missouri Medicaid.

  • Eligibility

    Eligibility is set by the program, not by us. We'll explain the general steps.

  • Verification

    Coverage is always verified before services start — no surprises.

  • Private pay & other options

    Other payment options can be discussed by phone.
