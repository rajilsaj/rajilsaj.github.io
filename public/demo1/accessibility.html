Accessibility Draft

A website everyone can use.

Vs Home Care and Nursing Care wants this website to be usable by everyone, including older adults and people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

What we have built in

  • Readable text sizes and strong color contrast.
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus outlines.
  • Labelled form fields with clear error messages.
  • Descriptive text for images.
  • Reduced animation for visitors who turn on reduced motion on their device.

Need help or found a problem?

If anything on this site is hard to use, please email vshomeinfocare@gmail.com. We are happy to help.
